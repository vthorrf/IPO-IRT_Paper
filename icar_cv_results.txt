ICAR (SAPA) cross-validated model comparison: EAP-plugin vs posterior-predictive IMV
Doria Xiao, July 2026. Produced by icar_cv_protocol.R (seed 7).

Data: IRW table icar_sapa (Condon & Revelle SAPA data). Subtests with a
documented 6-alternative format: Letter-Number (9 items) and Matrix
Reasoning (11 items). All respondents with at least one observed response
in the subtest; planned missingness ~63-73% within subtest (persons answer
2-3 items on average). 5-fold cross-validation by person.

Models per fold: Rasch, 2PL, 2.5PL (2PL with g fixed at 1/6) via mirt;
IPO-U and IPO-K via IPO-IRT Utilities.R (masked likelihood, defaults).

Each cell "A -> B" is the out-of-sample IMV of model B against baseline A
(positive favours B), averaged over folds, with the fold SD in parentheses.
Held-out predictions are leave-one-item-out, computed identically for all
models on a common theta grid, under two protocols:
  EAP-plugin : ICC evaluated at the person's EAP point estimate
  post-pred  : ICC integrated over the person's full posterior

Reading notes:
- The verdict on IPO-K vs Rasch reverses between protocols on both subtests.
  Under posterior-predictive evaluation the out-of-sample ranking agrees with
  in-sample BIC; under EAP-plugin it does not.
- IPO-K > IPO-U in every cell (both subtests, both protocols).
- The sanity pair Rasch -> 2PL is positive and stable under both protocols.
- hw: a real hesitation band on Letter-Number, essentially none on Matrix
  Reasoning.
